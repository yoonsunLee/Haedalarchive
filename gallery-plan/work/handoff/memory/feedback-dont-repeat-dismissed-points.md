When reviewing the user's print drafts (exhibition CV, preface, artist intro), once the user rejects a point or says to stop mentioning it, drop it entirely in later rounds — don't list it under "still remaining".

**Why:** The user explicitly told me to stop mentioning the CV list notation (missing comma after 《URBAN BREAK & TOYCON SEOUL》 단독 부스, no 《》 on 하나은행… / 세종시문화관광재단… items) after I repeated it across several rounds. They also chose to keep the CV section order despite my suggestion.

**How to apply:** Keep a mental list of settled decisions per artifact; re-reviews cover only new or still-open issues.

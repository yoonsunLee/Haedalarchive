The artist requires (2026-11-05) that the first work a visitor sees on entering the works hall is HD-2026-001, 「이 세계의 얼빵해달, 저작인접권자가 되었다!」 (45 × 45 cm).

Gallery Version 33 does this with FIRST_WORK in scratchpad g6-works.js: the work hangs alone on a freestanding "입구 가벽" facing the entrance from the artist room.

**Why:** an explicit artist instruction. It was restated as a rule that must survive later re-layouts.
**How to apply:** when the works hall is split into the 2×2 sections (법과 권리 → POV → 우주조약 → N차적), put HD-2026-001 straight ahead at the entrance of the first room (법과 권리). This overrides the curator advice to put 더 블루북 on that axis. Related: [[gallery-v31-preferred]].

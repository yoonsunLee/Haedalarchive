The user criticized me for reducing the artist's work and world to the idea that it comes from traditional materials (hanji, nacre) and fairy-tale picture books. Examples were hanji turning into picture-book paper, paper walls, and doors as book pages. The 얼빵해달 world is **contemporary society**:

- The IP page's first line: "현대사회에 살고 있는 조개배달부".
- POV works: commuting with an iced americano, working, clocking out, paid holidays — labour law, work rules.
- The 현대사회스포츠센터 morning swim class.
- Law-and-rights wit as contemporary art. 나전칠기 is the medium, not the theme.

The colour-pencil illustrations are a drawing style, not a "storybook world" to rebuild in materials.

**Why:** the IP관 entrance and transitions came out literal and off-world (2026-11-06).

**How to apply:**
- Frame IP관 spaces as "a day of a shell-delivery worker living in modern society".
- Draw transitions from modern everyday life (commute, delivery, sports centre), not hanji or book pages.
- Put this premise first in any expert brief.
